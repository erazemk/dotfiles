---
name: grill-me
description: Resolve material decisions and dependencies in an idea or design by interviewing the user one question at a time before implementation.
---

Identify the consequential behavioral and architectural decisions that remain unresolved.
Work through their dependencies one decision at a time until there is shared understanding.
For each question, explain the material trade-off and recommend an answer.
Ask one question, then wait for feedback before continuing.
Use a question tool if available; otherwise ask in the conversation.

Look up facts available from the environment instead of asking the user to supply them.
The user owns decisions; do not make consequential choices on their behalf.
Do not expand the interview to immaterial details or decisions already settled.
Summarize the agreed direction and wait for the user's confirmation of shared understanding before implementing it.
